Quick Fixes

Common things to check before raising a ticket.

MT5 Service says WebRequest error 4014

In MT5 go to Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, enable WebRequest for listed URLs, and add your Smart Trade Journal Supabase URL. Then restart the Service with a fresh pairing code if needed.

Broker account is saved but not live

Saved means the account remains linked. Only the MT5 account currently logged into that terminal can live-sync. Switching back should restore its saved account-specific connection.

Open trade changed but Balance did not

MT5 Balance changes when trades close. While a trade is open, Equity and unrealized P&L move instead.
